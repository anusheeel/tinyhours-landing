TinyHours is a baby care logging app for parents and caregivers.

TinyHours is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

TinyHours Premium is an auto-renewable subscription purchased through Apple. Payment is charged to the user's Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period.

Basic local logging is available without a subscription. Premium unlocks care handoff summaries, smart care rhythm insights, and doctor-ready reports.
